\begin{abstract}
\method reconstructs a frame-0 canonical Gaussian scene from a calibrated basketball-camera ring,
then tracks dynamic motion with group-aware control points, fused rigid moving-least-squares
(MLS), mask/centroid constraints, focused refinement, and periodic keyframes. The final
300-frame diagnostic produces novel-view videos and baked Gaussian checkpoints. All timing below
is measured on an RTX 3080 12 GB; RTX PRO 6000 fields are intentionally reserved for replication.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem and data}
The data contains 36 synchronized 4K ring cameras with fixed known calibration. Views
$0,3,\ldots,33$ train the model; the other 24 are held out. Camera poses are never optimized.
The raw-capture adapter accepts \texttt{cameras/view\_XXX.mp4} and
\texttt{calibration/cameras.txt}, \texttt{images.txt}, producing half-resolution pinhole inputs.

\section{Methodology}
\subsection{Canonical frame-0 reconstruction}
Stage A undistorts images, detects people, prompts SAM2 masks, and associates instances through
3D occupancy carving. For each person, an occlusion-aware visual hull intersects the backprojected
mask cones. A view does not carve a candidate voxel when a known foreground/static occluder
explains its missing silhouette. MASt3R runs in person-specific crops; matches are triangulated
with fixed cameras and rejected for poor reprojection, weak baseline, hull inconsistency, or
cross-view colour inconsistency. The accepted points initialize separate person Gaussian models
and a background model.

\subsection{Group-aware control-point MLS}
Each Gaussian is bound to its $K=8$ nearest controls from the \emph{same} group. Its fixed radial
basis weights are
\[
w_i(g)=\frac{\exp(-\|x_g-c_i\|^2/2\sigma^2)}{\sum_{j=1}^{K}\exp(-\|x_g-c_j\|^2/2\sigma^2)}.
\]
Thus player splats cannot be pulled by other players or background controls. At each frame the
optimizer estimates one shared translation per movable group plus local control offsets. A fused
CUDA rigid-MLS kernel transforms Gaussian means, orientations, and degree-1 SH coefficients.
The loss combines $L_1+$D-SSIM, per-instance mask BCE, ARAP using local MLS rotations, temporal
acceleration, and triangulated mask-centroid anchors. Dynamic-only deformation freezes the
background controls between keyframes and composites dynamic splats over a cached background.

\subsection{Focused refinement}
After every non-keyframe deformation, dynamic Gaussians are projected into every camera. Their
padded, tile-aligned union crop is refined for 100 Gaussian iterations without densification.
Every tenth frame receives 500 full-image iterations with bounded densification and a
Gaussian-control rebind. The model state is baked forward after each frame.

\subsection{Pseudocode}
\begin{algorithm}[H]
\caption{CourtFlow-GS online reconstruction}\label{alg:courtflow}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require Frame-0 images, synchronized training videos, fixed calibration
\State $(\mathit{state},\mathit{controls}) \gets \Call{BuildCanonicalScene}{\mathit{frame}_0,\mathit{calibration}}$
\For{each $f$ in the video sequence}
  \State $\mathit{images} \gets \Call{DecodeUndistortNormalize}{\mathit{trainingVideos}, f}$
  \If{$f \bmod 5 = 0$}
    \State $\mathit{masks} \gets \Call{SAM2RePrompt}{\mathit{images},\Call{Project}{\mathit{state.dynamic}}}$
    \State $\mathit{anchors} \gets \Call{Triangulate}{\Call{MaskCentroids}{\mathit{masks}}}$
  \Else
    \State $\mathit{masks},\mathit{anchors} \gets \varnothing,\varnothing$
  \EndIf
  \State $\mathit{offsets} \gets \Call{OptimizeControls}{L_1{+}\mathrm{D\text{-}SSIM},\mathit{masks},\mathit{anchors},\mathrm{ARAP},\mathrm{temporal}}$
  \State $\mathit{state} \gets \Call{Bake}{\Call{FusedRigidMLS}{\mathit{state},\mathit{controls},\mathit{offsets}}}$
  \If{$f \bmod 10 \ne 0$}
    \State $\mathit{state} \gets \Call{Refine}{\Call{DynamicContentCrops}{\mathit{state},\mathit{images}},100,\mathit{noDensify}}$
  \Else
    \State $\mathit{state} \gets \Call{Refine}{\mathit{images},500,\mathit{boundedDensify}}$
    \State $\mathit{controls} \gets \Call{Rebind}{\mathit{state},\mathit{controls}}$; \Call{CacheBackground}{\mathit{state}}
  \EndIf
  \State \Call{SaveDiagnostics}{\mathit{state}}
\EndFor
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

The fused MLS/SVD path is a small fraction of tracking time; rasterization forward/backward
dominates optimization. Baked-state GPU rendering is fast, while Python-to-ffmpeg transfer limits
the current encoded-video exporter.

\section{Limitations and next steps}
Crowded late-game plays cause independent per-camera SAM2 identities to swap or disappear. In the
final diagnostic, training IoU falls from 0.789 near the start to 0.001 at frame 299; held-out
person PSNR falls from 23.86 to 20.59 dB. The highest-priority improvement is detector-led,
multi-view identity association at keyframes. Additional work includes checkpoint consolidation or
LOD for the 2.01M-Gaussian final state, unbiased inter-keyframe held-out evaluation, and direct
GPU-to-NVENC output.
