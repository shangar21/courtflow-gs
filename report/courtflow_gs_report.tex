\begin{abstract}
\method reconstructs a frame-0 canonical Gaussian scene from a calibrated basketball-camera ring,
then tracks dynamic motion with group-aware control points, fused rigid moving-least-squares
(MLS), mask/centroid constraints, focused refinement, and periodic keyframes. The final
300-frame diagnostic produces novel-view videos and baked Gaussian checkpoints. All timing below
is measured on an RTX 3080 12 GB; RTX PRO 6000 fields are intentionally reserved for replication.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem and data}
The data contains 36 synchronized 4K ring cameras with fixed known calibration. Views
$0,3,\ldots,33$ train the model; the other 24 are held out. Camera poses are never optimized.
The raw-capture adapter accepts \texttt{cameras/view\_XXX.mp4} and
\texttt{calibration/cameras.txt}, \texttt{images.txt}, producing half-resolution pinhole inputs.

\section{Methodology}
This section specifies the reported configuration so that the method can be recreated without
reading the implementation.  Let $P_c=K_c[R_c\mid t_c]$ be a fixed undistorted pinhole camera,
with world-to-camera convention $x_c=R_cx+t_c$.  Camera poses, intrinsics, and photometric gains
are never optimized. RGB is divided by 255.  The 12 training cameras are every third camera of the
36-camera ring; all other cameras are held out. Every input is undistorted before segmentation,
matching, training, or evaluation.

\subsection{Canonical frame-0 reconstruction}
\paragraph{Masks and 3D identities.}
On frame 0, Faster R-CNN detects people (score $\geq0.5$) and SAM2 refines every on-court box;
masks smaller than 64 pixels are discarded. Identities are established in 3D rather than by
chaining 2D detections. Fit the court plane with RANSAC on full-frame MASt3R triangulations and,
when needed, estimate metric scale from a 1.9 m person-height prior. Make a court-aligned grid of
5 cm voxels, 2.4 m high, with a 1 m horizontal margin. Keep a voxel if it projects into the union
foreground mask in at least $\max(4,n_{\mathrm{visible}}-2)$ cameras. Form 26-connected components,
reject components with fewer than 30 voxels, height below 1.2 m, or a bottom implausibly above the
floor, and split a footprint exceeding 1.1 m into
$\lceil\mathrm{maximum\ footprint\ extent}/1.1\rceil$ floor-plane k-means clusters. Sort components
by floor $(u,v)$ to assign deterministic IDs.

Project each component into each camera, splat its projected coarse voxels to a silhouette, and
Hungarian-match detector masks to those silhouettes. Accept matches only at IoU $\geq0.20$. For an
unmatched component, re-prompt SAM2 with its projected box and centroid. Accept this recovery only
if its 16-by-16 HSV histogram has Bhattacharyya distance $\leq0.5$ from the median histogram of
that component's accepted masks in other views. A failed recovery leaves the camera \emph{unlabelled}
for that identity---it is not background evidence. Resolve overlapping accepted masks by nearest
median component depth. This yields mutually exclusive integer labels $L_c$ (0 is background) and
median label depths $d_{c,k}$.

\paragraph{Occlusion-aware visual hull.}
For identity $k$, make a 1 cm grid in the coarse-component bounds plus two coarse-voxel margins.
Camera $c$ gives a candidate voxel $x$ a negative vote only if $x$ is in its frustum, $c$ has an
accepted label for $k$, $L_c(\pi_c(x))\ne k$, and no nearer reconstructed surface explains the
absence. The absence is explained if either (i) the pixel belongs to another labelled identity $j$
with $d_{c,j}<d_{c,k}$, or (ii) a static depth map obeys
$D_c(\pi_c(x))<z_c(x)-0.30$ m. Build $D_c$ from full-frame MASt3R points labelled background and
colour-consistent in at least two extra views, keeping nearest depth after a 4-pixel square splat;
exclude points within 0.5 m of the floor.

Retain $x$ iff it is observed inside $k$'s label in at least two cameras and has zero unexplained
negative votes; then remove voxels below the floor. Therefore a missing silhouette pixel is not
forgiven merely because occlusion is possible: it is forgiven only when a measured nearer dynamic
or static depth demonstrates an occluder. An unlabelled camera casts no vote. This is the precise
meaning of ``occlusion-aware'' in this method.

\paragraph{Per-instance crop MASt3R points and Gaussian seeds.}
For every identity and each adjacent/skip-one ring pair $(c,c+1)$ and $(c,c+2)$, use the tight
label box, enlarge its side by 35\%, enforce a 96-pixel minimum, clip by translation to the image,
and resize the square crop to $512\times512$. Dilate the source mask by two pixels and query
MASt3R reciprocal nearest neighbours only at every second source-mask pixel. Map crop coordinates
back with $u=(u_{512}+0.5)s^{-1}-0.5+x_0$ (and analogously for $v$). Retain a correspondence iff
confidence is $\geq1.5$, both endpoints lie in their identity masks, both depths are positive,
maximum reprojection error is below 1.5 pixels, triangulation angle is at least $3^\circ$, and the
DLT point lies in the one-voxel-dilated hull. It must also be label-consistent and have mean RGB
$L_1<0.12$ in at least one non-anchor camera.

Those points are accurate surface seeds. Add only surface hull voxels farther than two hull voxels
from a seed, thereby filling weak texture without duplicating matched surfaces. Assign each point
the median colour across label-consistent self-z-buffer-visible views, remove statistical outliers
(20 neighbours, 2 standard deviations), and fuse at 5 mm for people. Background uses full-frame
matches outside person hulls dilated by 10 cm and the same colour test, fused at 10 cm. Initialize
one normal-oriented, anisotropic degree-1-SH Gaussian per fused point with opacity 0.5, a
third-neighbour scale, and normal-axis scale 0.1 times tangent scale. Persons train for 7000
iterations (four cameras/step) and background for 10000 (two cameras/step), using
\[
\mathcal L_{\rm image}=\|I-\hat I\|_1+0.2\,\mathrm{D\mbox{-}SSIM}
+0.1\mathcal L_{\rm alpha}+0.05\mathcal L_{\rm depth}.
\]
Depth weight decays to zero halfway through training. Densification starts at iteration 500 and
runs every 100 iterations until halfway; prune opacity below 0.01 and splats outside a
3-pixel-dilated mask in two views.

\subsection{Group-aware control-point MLS}
Every dynamic Gaussian belongs to exactly one person (the ball, when enabled, is a separate
group); background is group 0. Farthest-point sample 1024 controls per person, 16 for the ball,
and 1000 for background. Each Gaussian is bound once to its $K=8$ nearest controls from the
\emph{same} group. Its fixed radial-basis weights are
\[
w_{ij}=\frac{\exp(-\|x_i-p_j\|^2/(2\sigma_j^2))}
{\sum_{\ell=1}^{8}\exp(-\|x_i-p_\ell\|^2/(2\sigma_\ell^2))},
\]
where $\sigma_j$ is the mean distance from control $j$ to its three nearest controls. The six
nearest same-group controls form the ARAP graph. Thus a player's splats cannot be moved by any
other player or by background controls.

At frame $f$, warm-start control offsets from the previous control velocity. Optimize a shared
translation $T_k$ per movable group and local control offsets $\delta_j$ for 50 Adam iterations,
using four cameras/iteration and learning rates 0.020 m and 0.003 m respectively. Let
$t_j=T_{g(j)}+\delta_j$, $p'_j=p_j+t_j$, and retain the fixed weights above. For Gaussian $i$,
\[
 \bar p_i=\sum_jw_{ij}p_j,\quad \bar q_i=\sum_jw_{ij}p'_j,\quad
 M_i=\sum_jw_{ij}(p_j-\bar p_i)(p'_j-\bar q_i)^T.
\]
With $M_i=USV^T$, set $R_i=VU^T$, flipping the last column of $V$ if $\det(VU^T)<0$. The baked
position is $x'_i=R_i(x_i-\bar p_i)+\bar q_i$; orientation and degree-1 SH are also rotated by
$R_i$. The fused CUDA operation evaluates this transform and its backward pass. The reported run
does not blend separately optimized control rotations.

Every fifth frame, render per-instance weights, prompt SAM2 with each rendered box and centroid,
and resolve overlap by highest rendered instance weight. Triangulate each mask centroid by
area-square-root-weighted least-squares ray intersection. Use the resulting displacement to
initialize $T_k$, and apply a Huber anchor of scale 0.3 m and weight 0.1 when enough views observe
the identity. On intervening frames, no mask or anchor is fabricated. The deformation objective is
\[
\mathcal L=\mathcal L_{\rm image}+0.3\mathcal L_{\rm BCE}^{\rm instance}
+\mathcal L_{\rm ARAP}+0.01\mathcal L_{\rm accel}+\mathcal L_{\rm anchor},
\]
where ARAP averages $\|(p'_a-p'_b)-R_a(p_a-p_b)\|^2/\|p_a-p_b\|^2$ over graph edges and
acceleration is normalized second-order control displacement. During deformation, background
controls are frozen and the dynamic raster is depth-composited over a cached background.

\subsection{Focused refinement}
Bake Gaussian and control state forward after every deformation. At a non-keyframe, project all
dynamic Gaussians into every training camera, form their union box with 48 pixels of padding,
snap it outward to the 16-pixel gsplat tile grid, shift the principal point by the crop origin, and
run 100 refinement iterations on two crops/step. Tile alignment makes crop rasterization
pixel-identical to the corresponding full render. This phase has no densification or pruning, so
control bindings remain stable. Every tenth frame instead runs 500 full-image iterations, permits
densification from iteration 50 every 50 steps until 60\% of the schedule, caps growth at 5\% per
keyframe, rebinds each Gaussian to eight nearest same-group controls, and refreshes the background
cache. Held-out cameras are never used in a loss.

\subsection{Pseudocode}
\begin{algorithm}[H]
\caption{CourtFlow-GS online reconstruction}\label{alg:courtflow}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require Frame-0 images, synchronized training videos, fixed calibration
\State $(\mathit{state},\mathit{controls}) \gets \textsc{BuildCanonicalScene}(\mathit{frame}_0,\mathit{calibration})$
\For{each $f$ in the video sequence}
  \State $\mathit{images} \gets \textsc{DecodeUndistortNormalize}(\mathit{trainingVideos},f)$
  \If{$f \bmod 5 = 0$}
    \State $\mathit{masks} \gets \textsc{SAM2RePrompt}(\mathit{images},\mathit{state.dynamic})$
    \State $\mathit{anchors} \gets \textsc{TriangulateMaskCentroids}(\mathit{masks})$
  \Else
    \State $\mathit{masks},\mathit{anchors} \gets \emptyset,\emptyset$
  \EndIf
  \State $\mathit{offsets} \gets \textsc{OptimizeControls}(L_1{+}\mathrm{D\text{-}SSIM},\mathit{masks},\mathit{anchors},\mathrm{ARAP})$
  \State $\mathit{state} \gets \textsc{BakeFusedRigidMLS}(\mathit{state},\mathit{controls},\mathit{offsets})$
  \If{$f \bmod 10 \ne 0$}
    \State $\mathit{state} \gets \textsc{RefineDynamicCrops}(\mathit{state},\mathit{images},100)$
  \Else
    \State $\mathit{state} \gets \textsc{RefineFullImages}(\mathit{state},\mathit{images},500)$
    \State $\mathit{controls} \gets \textsc{Rebind}(\mathit{state},\mathit{controls})$; \textsc{CacheBackground}($\mathit{state}$)
  \EndIf
  \State \textsc{SaveDiagnostics}($\mathit{state}$)
\EndFor
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

the current encoded-video exporter.

\section{Limitations and next steps}
Crowded late-game plays cause independent per-camera SAM2 identities to swap or disappear. In the
final diagnostic, training IoU falls from 0.789 near the start to 0.001 at frame 299; held-out
person PSNR falls from 23.86 to 20.59 dB. The highest-priority improvement is detector-led,
multi-view identity association at keyframes. Additional work includes checkpoint consolidation or
LOD for the 2.01M-Gaussian final state, unbiased inter-keyframe held-out evaluation, and direct
GPU-to-NVENC output.
