Attached is my CourtFlow-GS submission for the reconstruction take-home assignment.

The ZIP contains:
- courtflow_gs_report.pdf — method, evaluation protocol, held-out metrics, runtime, limitations,
  and the development/failure analysis;
- videos/ — final novel-view renders from held-out cameras and an orbit, plus an uncapped comparison;
- metrics/ — final-run summaries;
- code/ — the complete source, including the forward-only CUDA playback command
  `python -m ring_init.play --package <playback_dir> --camera view:13 --out view13.mp4`.

The final reported result uses 12 of 36 cameras for training and evaluates only on the other 24
cameras: 20.43 dB mean held-out PSNR over 700 frames at 1080p. It was trained on one RTX PRO 6000.

The portable playback exporter is included in the source. A final model package is produced by
running Stage B with `"v2_export_playback": true`; it is self-contained (calibration plus Gaussian
parameters) and can be copied to another CUDA-capable machine without the dataset or training stack.
